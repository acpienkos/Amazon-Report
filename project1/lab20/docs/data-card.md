# Data card and as-of boundary

**Prices.** `data/prices.csv` is the existing Lab 14 Yahoo Finance adjusted-close snapshot. [Source URLs and UTC retrieval times](../data/price_provenance.json) record October 20, 2026. Bars are labeled with the first day of their completed month. October 2021 to September 2026 gives 60 aligned prices and 59 returns. The October 20 research snapshot is after retrieval. Adjusted prices measure historical fund performance including distribution adjustments. They can be revised by the vendor and are not historical-vintage data. This is a single-date research estimate with no claim of vintage-correct price backtesting. There is no forward fill. The file hash manifest canonicalizes line endings.

**BAA.** Moody's Seasoned Baa Corporate Bond Yield, distributed by FRED/ALFRED. It is a monthly yield in percent, not seasonally adjusted. The [September 30 vintage](https://alfred.stlouisfed.org/graph/alfredgraph.csv?id=BAA&vintage_date=2026-09-30) was archived in Lab 17 on October 29. On this research snapshot the model deliberately uses the last verified month-end archive dated before October 20. The last observed month admitted is August 2026 (6.32%). September's 6.47% appears in the later October 1 archive and is excluded from this frozen policy. The cutoff conservatively avoids assuming source intraday timestamps. This signal is admitted only for this frozen retrospective research snapshot. A live or repeated strategy still requires dated archive coverage, release and trading-time checks and verification of the exact vintage selection. See [Lab 17's timing and revisions audit](../../../lab17/DATA_CARD.md).

**Decision use.** As a proposed risk overlay, BAA at or above 6% tightens the annualized volatility cap from 5% to 4%. The threshold and caps are analyst scenario settings, not fitted predictive relationships or client-approved rules. The engine refuses a same-day or more-than-30-day-old archive, a missing vintage, invalid data, and observations more than 90 days old using their month labels. It does not fall back to FRED's current file. The historical price dataset and date-level ALFRED archive serve distinct roles.
